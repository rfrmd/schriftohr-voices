SchriftOhr Voices

This is SchriftOhr's mirror of the Kokoro neural-voice assets — the CoreML model bundles, pronunciation lexicons, and voice packs the app downloads at first run. It exists so the voices are never a lost item: the app fetches from here first, with the original Hugging Face repo as automatic fallback.

Files are served in the Hub's own URL shapes under FluidInference/kokoro-82m-coreml/resolve/main/…, for example the default voice pack. Source and scope: github.com/rfrmd/schriftohr-voices.

Upstream: FluidInference's CoreML conversion of hexgrad's Kokoro-82M, Apache-2.0, redistributed unmodified.
